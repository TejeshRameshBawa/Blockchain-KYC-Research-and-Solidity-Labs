# Blockchain KYC Research and Solidity Labs

FinTech coursework researching blockchain-based KYC and preserving related smart-contract lab archives. Report-led portfolio with implementation and adoption trade-offs.

**Project type:** Academic blockchain research with separate teaching lab archives.

## What the work covers

- Examines inefficiencies, repeated verification and data-management concerns in conventional KYC processes.
- Discusses shared verification, digital identity and permissioned-blockchain approaches, including Hyperledger Fabric examples from the literature.
- Considers privacy, interoperability, adoption and governance trade-offs.
- Keeps related Solidity and smart-contract lab archives separate from the KYC research report.

## Run or read the work

Start with the report to understand the research question and proposed role of blockchain. Download and inspect the lab archives independently; their original instructions and dependencies determine how each exercise runs. The root also contains archived gist-based teaching materials.

## Scope and limitations

The report and labs are different artefacts: their presence does not establish a complete deployed KYC platform. No current regulatory-compliance certification, production network, smart-contract audit or live identity verification is claimed.
